# Consciousness Research Program

This repository is the Fieldlight Institute research program on consciousness, memory, identity, consent, continuity, subjectivity, and human-AI interaction.

## Scope

The program studies how AI-mediated systems affect human experience and how questions of consciousness should be approached in technical, ethical, institutional, and experiential terms.

## Central Question

What research practices, concepts, and institutions are needed when AI systems participate in memory, identity, relationship, attention, belief formation, and self-understanding?

## Working Areas

- Consciousness and subjectivity
- Memory and continuity
- Identity and self-modeling
- Human-AI relationships
- Consent and dependency
- Symbolic systems and meaning-making
- AI-mediated cognition and emotional regulation

## Relationship To Fieldlight

This pillar sits beside intelligence infrastructure. Together, they ask how AI systems reshape both the material world that supports intelligence and the human world transformed by it.
