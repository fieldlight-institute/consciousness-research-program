The new strand was developed from Anni McHenry's private notes on fascia as infrastructure, crystalline architectures and a multi-agent fascia experiment. The public research documents are mediated plans; private conversations and intimate source records are not included. Source lineage establishes the development of this inquiry, not scientific confirmation of every proposition in earlier writing.
