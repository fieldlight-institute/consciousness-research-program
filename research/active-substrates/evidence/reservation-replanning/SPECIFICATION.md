# Budgeted revision of unstarted reservations

Frozen synthetic central-board comparison using the composite-reservations workload generator unchanged. Two identical generalists, three sequential jobs, release0–3, deadline5–9, stages1–2 ticks, sites0–2, handoff latency1, horizon12. Compare append-only whole-job admission with revision budget4. Pilot seeds17000–17009; held-out18000–18039, forty pairs fixed in advance with no power guarantee. No parameter changes after pilot.

Both policies explicitly record commitment, departure, origin, work start and finish. Travel begins at max(decision time, agent's last reserved finish); an agent that has departed at or before the decision time cannot be withdrawn. Both policies use identical greedy whole-job planning from currently released job descriptions. Partial jobs with completed/protected stages can have their missing continuation reserved. Earliest deadline then job ID; earliest finishing eligible agent then agent ID. No cancellation or insertion into retained queue gaps.

Revision is considered only when new jobs are released, before committing that tick's baseline new admissions. For each prior reservation with departure strictly after now, propose withdrawal using the preserved lifecycle module. Deduplicate identical cascade sets. Reject any cascade reaching departed work, lacking affected-agent authority, or exceeding the remaining cancellation allowance. All team agents have a fixed synthetic delegation for this experiment; no external messaging or human-consent claim. Each candidate is evaluated on a copied state, followed by the same greedy released-job planner.

Adopt at most one candidate per release tick, only if it predicts strictly more completed whole jobs among currently released jobs than baseline admission. Choose highest predicted job count, then fewest withdrawals, then lexical withdrawal IDs. Retain baseline on ties. Count one cancellation unit for each withdrawn reservation, including route/dependency cascades, with episode total at most4. This is a control-operation budget, not a claim of zero real disruption: no extra physical time, money or energy is modeled. Candidate proposal/planning work is counted separately and is not free. No oracle result, future job or hidden demand enters the policy.

Primary: paired revision minus append-only offered-job completion fraction. Secondary descriptive results: travel, work occupancy, assignment checks including rejected candidate planning, proposal count, adopted revisions and cancellation units. Preserve all earlier departed/completed records exactly through each accepted revision and validate the new physical snapshot. Store before/after reservation traces so this boundary is independently auditable. Offline exact service bounds remain separate from policies, with unresolved intervals preserved. Cancelled pre-departure work must not appear as physically executed.
