# PA-01: local estimates and participant coupling authority

Frozen before pilot. A bounded implementation of Extension A in the October 20 generative-participation plan. This is synthetic operational recognition, not conscious recognition. All conditions retain AM-01 adaptive spatial edges and its 96 directed transfers/tick, task generator, agent skills, refusal, exploration, removal and scoring policy. Participant coupling is an additional scalar on the agent–medium boundary: it multiplies congestion read into scoring and the agent's own .5 claim deposit. It does not alter shared-edge conductance directly. Bounds .25–1.75, initial 1. Medium deposits remain clipped at 1. No abstention, new refusal or departure mechanism is added; inherited prohibited-skill refusal is preserved.

Five recipient conditions: R0A0, R1A0, R0A1, R1A1, Yoked. R gives access to a local reliability estimate for revision; A permits self-adjustment. Estimate-only R1A0 deliberately has no other route to affect behavior and is an exact negative control. R0A1 follows a prespecified clock-driven triangular comparator, independent of its reliability. Yoked replays the same agent IDs' coupling trajectory from an R1A1 donor with seed=recipient seed+100000, an independent task realization with identical generator, time phases and skills. Recipient history cannot change the replay. This tests transferring an independently learned schedule, not a perfect causal isolation of recognition: donor environment differences can harm portability. Donor run cost is reported separately.

At commitment store the raw destination field. On own arrival, append a binary agreement: field >=.25 predicts failure; field <.25 predicts success. Maintain the most recent 32 agreements per agent. Reliability is Beta(1+agreements,1+disagreements) mean, with posterior standard deviation reported as descriptive uncertainty, not calibrated confidence under dependent observations. No unchosen outcomes or global truth enter this estimate. It measures predictive association, not causal benefit or sender honesty.

Every 25 ticks, before arrivals/decisions, scan each active agent's 32-slot allowance. With at least eight observations, R1A1 targets coupling1.5 if mean>.6, .5 if mean<.4, otherwise1; with fewer observations target1. Move at most .1 toward target. R0A1 moves .1 toward alternating1.5/.5 targets, switching each ten checkpoints with agent ID phase. R0A0/R1A0 remain1. Yoked applies the donor value at that checkpoint. Removed agents retain their last coupling. At2400 clear individual reliability windows and pending signal receipts, retain participant couplings as physical boundary state, as well as positions and work in flight. Subsequent inherited trips without receipts give no reliability update. Shared AM-01 eligibility reset is unchanged.

All conditions receive the same kinds of observations and reserve identical capacity; counterfactual trajectories naturally differ. All compute both summaries and comparator proposals at checkpoints; access/action gating is then enforced. Count actual window entries scanned, reserved32-slot scans, summaries, arrivals recorded, coupling writes, read/deposit multiplications, plus AM-01 operations separately. This supplies equal observation/representation and scheduled scan allowances, not exact CPU/energy equality. Estimate computation in R0 is inaccessible instrumentation. Donor generation is extra offline cost. Persistent capacity: 384 agreement bits, 12 coupling scalars, up to12 pending raw field values in addition to AM-01 state. Counters do not price interpreter/allocation overhead.

Primary endpoint: R1A1 minus Yoked completion of eligible jobs born1000–1499. Secondary: factorial interaction (R1A1−R1A0)−(R0A1−R0A0), R1A1−R0A0, travel, failed arrivals, coupling changes and uncertainty. Retain all recipient completion events for later recovery without rerunning. Stress exposure, time-shuffled estimates and explicit revision latency remain separate unfinished controls. Do not label the full extension complete.
