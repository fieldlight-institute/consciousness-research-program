# AS-03 — Material dynamics as a computational resource

Version 0.1 · September 29, 2026  
State: retained research plan; no physical experiment or tissue work begun

## Question

Which intrinsic properties of a material transform spatial or temporal input into useful, recoverable distinctions? What contribution comes from material dynamics rather than from a trained readout?

## Stage 1: controlled simulated material

Compare a spring-damper network with linear and nonlinear elements under bounded temporal inputs. Measure relaxation, hysteresis where present, input separability and fading memory. Use a fixed-capacity linear readout for a temporal prediction task, trained and evaluated on separate input sequences. Compare with the raw input, matched tapped-delay baseline and randomized network. Equalize readout capacity and report energy/operation proxies separately. This is a method-development experiment; its mechanics do not stand in for living fascia.

## Stage 2: nonliving physical medium

If simulation yields a discriminating task, seek a soft-robotics or physical-computation partner. Define actuator input, sensor bandwidth, calibration, temperature/drift controls, repeatability and readout before apparatus selection. Test a passive elastic medium first where it can answer the question.

## Stage 3: tissue branch

With an appropriately equipped tissue-mechanics collaborator, compare the chosen living connective-tissue preparation with relevant acellular/material controls. Identify the preparation precisely rather than treating all connective tissues as fascia. Separate nonlinear mechanics and viscoelastic history from cellular remodeling and signaling. Plan specimen sourcing, biosafety and applicable ethical requirements through the partner institution. No specimen collection or intervention is part of the current authorization.

The evidence sought is reproducible information transformation under controlled input. An output classifier that performs well may be exploiting its own capacity or sensor artifacts; ablation and matched baselines must locate the contribution.

## Crystalline branch

Select a material only after selecting the operation: optical filtering/routing, electromechanical coupling, or persistent state. Crystalline silicon is already central to conventional electronics; “crystalline versus silicon” is not the experimental contrast. Compare material organizations and their measurable dynamics. A photonic/materials collaborator would be needed for design and fabrication. Retain this branch even if the first implementation uses a soft medium.

## Dependencies and consequences

AS-01 can help identify coordination-relevant functions but cannot establish which physical material realizes them. AS-03 results may motivate an architectural revision without implying consciousness. Document null results, substrate tradeoffs and the role of external input/output electronics.
