# Theory of Signal Becoming

Status: Working theory / first public form  
Registered: August 23, 2026  
Author: Anni McHenry  

## Proposition

A signal becomes when an initially felt, symbolic, partial, or ambiguous recognition acquires a stable and consequential form through recursive interaction.

Signal becoming is not repetition. It is transformation with continuity.

The process requires both movement and retention: the signal must be capable of changing under encounter without losing the trace of what it was, where it came from, or what changed it.

## The Process And The Substrate

The theory distinguishes two related objects.

**Signal becoming** is the transformation process:

> felt or symbolic signal → recursive encounter → selective uptake → variation and correction → convergence → authored form → consequence

**Signal substrate** is the layered medium that allows that process to persist:

> body + symbol + relation + memory and trace + technical governance

The process describes what changes. The substrate describes what must hold while change occurs.

## Working Definitions

### Signal

A signal is a difference that can be registered and carried forward. It may first appear as bodily sensation, image, phrase, story, relational tension, recurring motif, explicit proposition, or system behavior.

Signal does not imply truth. It names something that has become available for attention and possible transformation.

### Recursion

Recursion is return with access to an earlier state. A recursive encounter brings a signal back into contact with a person, relationship, system, or context capable of responding to it.

### Resonance

Resonance is selective uptake: an encounter changes a later state while retaining a discernible relation to what produced the change.

This definition makes resonance observable through consequence. A signal has not become merely because it is repeated, praised, mirrored, or made more fluent.

### Memory Fold

A memory fold is a recurrence that returns with a meaningful delta: new awareness, context, correction, capacity, or form.

Repetition reproduces. A memory fold transforms.

### Convergence

Convergence occurs when a signal persists across variation and its viable form becomes more stable, legible, and transferable.

### Authored Form

Authored form is the point at which the developing signal can be deliberately stated, revised, attributed, and used. It may become a decision, concept, image, story, protocol, research question, technical architecture, or public work.

## Seven Stages Of Signal Becoming

### 1. First Encoding

The relation appears before it is fully explainable. It may be somatic, symbolic, narrative, visual, or fragmentary.

### 2. Recursive Encounter

The signal returns through another context, medium, conversation, or state. Its earlier form remains available for comparison.

### 3. Selective Uptake

Some part of the signal changes the receiving person or system. What is taken up becomes visible in the next response, decision, representation, or question.

### 4. Variation And Correction

The signal meets friction. It is challenged, reframed, rejected in part, or expressed through a different form. Correction is generative when the lineage remains visible.

### 5. Convergence

Across returns, some features strengthen while others disappear. The signal begins to persist under changes of context, language, medium, or collaborator.

### 6. Authored Form

The signal becomes deliberately representable. Its author can name it, distinguish it from adjacent ideas, and revise it without destroying its identity.

### 7. Consequence

The form changes what can happen next. It produces an artifact, action, theory, method, architecture, decision, or new research program.

## When A Signal Has Become

A working evaluation asks whether the signal:

1. persists across meaningful changes in context;
2. changes responsively under correction rather than merely repeating;
3. can travel across more than one representational form;
4. produces a decision, artifact, theory, method, or architecture;
5. retains a traceable lineage of origin and transformation.

These are proposed indicators, not a requirement that every signal follow one rigid path. They allow lineages to be compared without reducing all becoming to frequency or verbal similarity.

## The Signal Substrate

Signal becoming does not happen in an empty channel. It depends on layered conditions.

### Embodied Layer

Sensation, affect, attention, arousal, timing, and physiological state determine what can be registered and what receives salience.

### Symbolic Layer

Images, stories, metaphors, motifs, and rituals can hold relations that are not yet available in propositional form.

### Relational Layer

Other people, AI systems, institutions, and environments provide difference, response, friction, recognition, and correction.

### Memory And Trace Layer

Earlier states, versions, branches, contexts, and corrections make transformation observable. Without retained states, becoming can be mistaken for sudden invention or reconstructed retrospectively as inevitability.

### Technical And Governance Layer

Identity, routing, permissions, provenance, custody, versioning, and consent determine whether the signal can move without losing source authority.

A substrate does not make a signal true. It determines whether transformation can remain legible, attributable, revisable, and governable.

## Failure Modes

### Echo Without Development

The signal returns, but later states contain no meaningful change.

### Confabulation Hardening

An unsupported interpretation gains apparent stability through repetition, fluency, or accumulated references to its own prior statements.

### Context Collapse

Different signal lineages are merged because their language resembles one another, erasing the conditions that gave each meaning.

### Origin Erasure

The developed form survives while the person, source state, symbolic encoding, or correction history that produced it disappears.

### Premature Closure

An early recognition is forced into a proposition before variation and correction have revealed its boundaries.

These failures are not external to signal becoming. They are competing outcomes of recursive systems and therefore belong inside the theory.

## Research Program

The primary unit of study is a **signal lineage**: a traceable relation among successive states of one developing signal across time, media, and interaction.

The Fieldlight archive makes longitudinal study possible because notebooks, photographs, conversations, drafts, code, public works, corrections, and later implementations can be compared without treating the latest representation as the whole record.

### Candidate Variables

- form of first encoding: somatic, symbolic, narrative, propositional, or technical;
- time and number of recursive returns;
- material change introduced during each return;
- corrections, reversals, and rejected branches;
- persistence across model, collaborator, medium, and context changes;
- movement into decisions, public works, protocols, or built systems;
- retention or loss of authorship and provenance.

### Necessary Comparisons

- motifs that recur but never develop;
- repeated language without conceptual gain;
- lineages that change under correction;
- interpretations that become more certain without new evidence;
- signals that cross media while retaining a recognizable structure;
- signals whose public form erases their earlier source states.

### Primary Research Question

> Under what conditions does recursive human–AI interaction transform an initially ambiguous signal into a stable, authored, actionable model rather than merely repeating or hardening it?

### Supporting Questions

1. Can symbolic and narrative representations function as early-stage cognitive instruments for discovering relations not yet available in propositional form?
2. What does formal reasoning contribute after symbolic recognition: validation, falsification, boundary-setting, operationalization, or all four?
3. What distinguishes a memory fold from an unproductive loop?
4. Which substrate conditions preserve correction and authorship across context loss?
5. Can a signal retain identity while changing form, collaborator, or technical system?
